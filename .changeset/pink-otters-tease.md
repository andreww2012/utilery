Added new `writeJsonFile` and `writeJsonFileSync` utilities ([`writeJsonFile`](https://github.com/sindresorhus/write-json-file#api) and [`writeJsonFileSync`](https://github.com/sindresorhus/write-json-file#api) from [`write-json-file`](https://npmx.dev/write-json-file)) that write JSON atomically and keep the indentation of an existing file, and a new `getAppPaths` utility ([`envPaths`](https://github.com/sindresorhus/env-paths#api) from [`env-paths`](https://npmx.dev/env-paths)) for the data, config and cache directories of an app
