Added a new `formatDuration` utility from [`pretty-ms`](https://npmx.dev/pretty-ms) ([`prettyMilliseconds`](https://github.com/sindresorhus/pretty-ms#api)) that formats milliseconds like `'1h 2m 3s'` or, in colon notation, `'1:02:03'`, and a new `parseColonDuration` utility that parses the colon notation back into milliseconds
